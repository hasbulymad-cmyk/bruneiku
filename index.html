Key milestones

From vision to reality.

2008 Wawasan Brunei 2035 Proclaimed

His Majesty Sultan Hassanal Bolkiah officially announces the national long-term vision, setting targets for education, quality of life, and economic diversification by 2035.

2014 Supreme Council Established

A Supreme Council for Wawasan Brunei 2035 is created by royal decree to oversee, monitor, and drive implementation across all government ministries and agencies.

2016 Wawasan Framework & KPIs Introduced

A comprehensive measurement framework with 12 National Outcomes and Key Performance Indicators is launched to hold the nation accountable to progress.

2017 Non-Oil Exports: Just 3%

A stark baseline. Oil and gas dominate the economy almost entirely — making the diversification challenge clear, and setting the starting line for what comes next.

2020 Temburong Bridge Opens

The Sultan Haji Omar Ali Saifuddien Bridge connects Brunei-Muara and Temburong for the first time by road — a landmark infrastructure project symbolising national unity and opening Temburong's eco-tourism potential.

2023 Non-Oil Exports Reach 61%

One of the fastest economic diversification records in the region. From 3% to 61% in six years. The non-oil and gas sector now accounts for 56.6% of GDP — the target of below 30% hydrocarbon dependency is in sight.

2026 — Now The Critical Decade Begins

9 years remain. The next phase requires acceleration in education quality, digital economy growth, tourism development, and social infrastructure. This is where the vision meets delivery.

2035 Target: A Nation Transformed

Brunei Darussalam ranks among the world's top 10 nations for quality of life and GDP per capita — known globally for its educated people, sustainable economy, and preserved natural heritage.

Three aspirations

The pillars of Wawasan 2035.

Aspiration 01
Rakyat yang Berpendidikan dan Berkelayakan Tinggi
An Educated and Highly Skilled People

Brunei's people will be recognised globally for their accomplishments — measured against the highest international standards. Education that produces thinkers, innovators, and leaders capable of competing at the world stage.

World-class education outcomes by 2035
Aspiration 02
Kualiti Hidup yang Tinggi
Quality of Life Among the World's Top 10

Not just comfortable — exceptional. By 2035, Brunei aims to rank among the top 10 nations globally for quality of life, encompassing healthcare, safety, housing, environment, and the social fabric that makes a nation worth living in.

Top 10 globally for quality of life
Aspiration 03
Ekonomi yang Maju dan Mampan
A Dynamic and Sustainable Economy

GDP per capita among the world's top 10 — built on diversification, not dependence. Oil revenues reinvested into a resilient, knowledge-based economy that will thrive long after the wells run dry.

Top 10 globally for GDP per capita
How we get there

Eight national strategies.

Education

Prepare every Bruneian for a competitive, knowledge-based world. STEM, technical, and vocational excellence measured internationally.

Economy

Diversify beyond oil. Grow the private sector. Attract investment in downstream industries and non-hydrocarbon clusters.

Security

Maintain sovereignty and political stability. Build resilience against emerging threats — climate, health, and geopolitical.

Institutional Development

High-performing, transparent governance. A public sector that moves at the speed of the economy it serves.

Local Business

Grow Brunei's SME ecosystem through DARe and beyond. Reduce reliance on government employment. Build Bruneian entrepreneurs.

Infrastructure

World-class roads, ports, digital networks, and energy systems — including the landmark Temburong Bridge.

Social Security

Ensure every citizen benefits as the nation grows. Housing, healthcare, and a social safety net that leaves no one behind.

Environment

Protect Brunei's extraordinary natural heritage — 72% intact rainforest — while building a sustainable, green economy.

Economic diversification

Five priority sectors.

01
Downstream Oil & Gas
▼

Move beyond extraction into refining, petrochemicals, and LNG value-adding. Capturing significantly more economic value from existing reserves before they run dry — while building expertise that outlasts the oil itself.

02
Food & Agriculture
▼

National food security and agribusiness export. Aquaculture, halal food processing, and smart farming in Brunei's fertile interior. The goal: produce enough to feed the nation and export the surplus — especially to halal markets globally.

03
Tourism
▼

Eco-tourism in Temburong, cultural heritage in Kampong Ayer, MICE tourism and luxury hospitality in BSB. Brunei's pitch: Southeast Asia's most authentic, unhurried, and spiritually grounded destination. Small, exclusive, and genuinely different.

04
ICT & Digital Economy
▼

Smart nation infrastructure, digital government services, fintech, and data economy. Political stability and zero income tax on foreign earnings position Brunei as an unlikely but attractive base for technology companies looking for a Southeast Asian foothold.

05
Halal Industry & Financial Services
▼

Islamic finance, halal certification, and pharmaceutical manufacturing. The global halal economy is valued at over $7 trillion. Brunei's credentials — Islamic governance, trusted certification, and a Muslim-majority population — make it a natural hub.
