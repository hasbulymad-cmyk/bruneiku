Three aspirations

The pillars of Wawasan 2035.

Aspiration 01
🎓
Rakyat yang Berpendidikan dan Berkelayakan Tinggi
An Educated and Highly Skilled People

Brunei's people will be recognised globally for their accomplishments — measured by the highest international standards. Education that produces not just graduates, but thinkers, innovators, and leaders capable of competing and contributing at the world stage.

Aspiration 02
🌏
Kualiti Hidup yang Tinggi
Quality of Life Among the World's Top 10

Not just comfortable — exceptional. By 2035, Brunei aims to rank among the top 10 nations globally for quality of life. Healthcare, safety, housing, environment, and social cohesion all measured and improved with intention. A Brunei that the world would want to live in.

Aspiration 03
⚡
Ekonomi yang Maju dan Mampan
A Dynamic and Sustainable Economy

GDP per capita among the world's top 10 — built on diversification, not dependence. Oil and gas revenues reinvested into a resilient, knowledge-based economy that will thrive long after the wells run dry. Tourism, digital industries, halal, and green energy leading the way.

How we get there

Eight national strategies.

📚
Education

Prepare every Bruneian for a competitive, knowledge-based world — STEM, vocational, and beyond.

💼
Economy

Foster investment in non-oil sectors. Grow the private economy. Reduce hydrocarbon dependency below 30% of GDP.

🛡️
Security

Maintain sovereignty, political stability, and resilience against emerging threats — health, climate, and geopolitical.

🏛️
Institutional Development

Build a high-performing, transparent public sector. Good governance as a competitive advantage.

🏢
Local Business

Grow Brunei's SME ecosystem. Support entrepreneurs through DARe and reduce reliance on government employment.

🌉
Infrastructure

World-class roads, ports, digital networks, and utilities — including the Temburong Bridge linking Brunei's divided territory.

🤝
Social Security

Ensure every citizen benefits as the nation prospers. Housing, healthcare, and welfare that leaves no one behind.

🌿
Environment

Conserve Brunei's extraordinary natural heritage — 72% intact rainforest — while pursuing sustainable development.

Progress on the ground

Brunei is already moving.
The numbers prove it.

3% → 61% Non-oil exports share — from 2017 to 2023. One of the fastest diversification records in the region.
56.6% GDP now coming from non-oil & gas sectors. The target of below 30% hydrocarbon dependency is within reach.
72% Of Brunei's land area is intact tropical rainforest — one of the highest proportions on earth. A built-in advantage.
#1 Southeast Asia ranking for government subsidy per capita. Stability and welfare as a foundation for growth.
Economic diversification

Five priority sectors.

⛽
Downstream Oil & Gas

Move beyond extraction. Refining, petrochemicals, and LNG value-adding — capturing more economic value from existing reserves before they run out.

🌾
Food & Agriculture

National food security and agribusiness export. Aquaculture, halal food processing, and smart farming in Brunei's fertile interior districts.

✈️
Tourism

Eco-tourism in Temburong, cultural heritage in Kampong Ayer, MICE tourism in BSB. Positioning Brunei as Southeast Asia's most authentic, unhurried destination.

💻
ICT & Digital Economy

Smart nation infrastructure, digital services, fintech, and data economy. Building the platform for a knowledge-based economy that exports intellectual value.

🌙
Halal Industry & Financial Services

Islamic finance, halal certification, and pharmaceutical manufacturing. Brunei as a trusted global hub for the $7 trillion halal economy.
